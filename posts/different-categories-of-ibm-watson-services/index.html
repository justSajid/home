Different Categories of IBM Watson Services

Published June 11, 2017 in IBM Watson by justSajid 1 min read
Table of Contents

Well, on the basis of behavior of IBM Watson Services available on Bluemix Cloud, you can simply categories IBM Watson services as bellow;

Utility

  • Speech-to-text
  • Text-to-speech
  • Document conversion

Non-Trainable

  • Tone analyzer
  • Personality Insights

Trainable: (User data)

  • Conversation
  • Natural language classifier
  • Discovery
  • Retrieve and rank

Trainable: (Enhanced Functionality)

  • Natural language understanding
  • Visual recognition
  • Language translator
  • X (Twitter)
  • Facebook
  • LinkedIn
  • WhatsApp
